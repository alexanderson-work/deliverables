## Why they aren't video

The obvious way to put a product demo on a marketing site is to record the app and drop in an MP4. That was tried and rejected, for two reasons:

- **These mockups are roughly 85% small text** — stat labels, row names, amounts, all between 12 and 18px. Video compression smears text at that size, and it looks especially bad next to the crisp type everywhere else on the page.
- **Transparency does not survive.** Video with an alpha channel fails in Safari, and video without one carries its own background, which would sit as a visible rectangle on the navy page.

The cost is that a native demo needs its own type sizing per breakpoint, where a video would simply scale. That is why several of these exist as a desktop and a mobile pair.

The largest of them: a full dashboard mockup inside the tabbed showcase below the hero.

| Component | Width | Used at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Demo** | `1072px` fixed | Desktop **and tablet** |
| **Product Demo Mobile** | `352px` fixed | Phone only |

Both have the same three variants: **Payments**, **Payables**, and **Beyond Payments**.

<Callout>
  <p>They have to be two separate components because <strong>a component variant cannot have a percentage or flexible width</strong> — Framer requires a fixed pixel value on a variant root. A fixed-width component does not shrink to fit its container; it clips. The <code>1072px</code> is measured to fit its slot exactly, so <strong>if you change the showcase's width or padding, the demo will clip until you re-measure it.</strong></p>
</Callout>

### How one is built

A horizontal stack: a **Sidebar** (`230px`, logo plus nine menu items) and a **Content** column that fills the rest, holding four stat tiles and then the body.

The clever part is the body. **All three bodies exist in every variant as siblings**, and each variant simply hides two of them:

| Variant | Visible body | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payments | Body — Payments | A **feed** — uniform transaction rows |
| Payables | Body — Payables | A **flow** — a batch total splitting across rails into destinations |
| Beyond Payments | Body — Beyond Payments | A **portfolio** — a revenue chart over a grid of product cards |

An early version made all three look nearly identical, and the feedback was exactly that. The fix was a principle worth keeping:

> **Keep the shared chrome identical, and give each demo the body shape its own story needs.**

The sidebar and the four stat tiles are what make the three read as one product. The body is what makes them read as three different things. They are distinguishable even as thumbnails — a list, a branching flow, and a chart over cards.

There is also no separate "active state" layer in the sidebar. The highlighted menu item is just a per-variant fill and text color override.

### The mobile version

<Figure src="/clients/justifi/guides/animated-product-demos/2.png" alt="Product Demo Mobile, Payments variant: a narrow icon rail in place of the sidebar, the four stat tiles in a 2×2 grid, payment-method chips, and transaction rows marked with small colored status dots instead of pills." caption="Product Demo Mobile, Payments variant — 352px wide" />

### How they're wired in

The demos sit inside the tabbed **Solutions Showcase** (`1120px`, desktop and tablet) and **Solutions Showcase Mobile** (`400px`, phone). Each showcase has three variants, one per tab; switching a tab switches the demo with it.

<Callout>
  <p><strong>Tablet uses the desktop showcase, not the mobile one.</strong> Only phone swaps components. The demo reflows to roughly 682px at tablet, which is why the stat tiles are a <strong>grid</strong> with a minimum column width rather than a fixed four-across row, and why every row of text has truncation enabled — without those, numbers break mid-figure and names break mid-word.</p>
</Callout>

  <p><strong>Worth a cleanup pass before launch.</strong> That is six hidden visuals carrying real weight in the file. ## 5. The Onboarding Matrix

On `/sub-account-onboarding`. Not a product mockup but built the same way — a native, animated data view rather than an image of a table.

<Figure src="/clients/justifi/guides/animated-product-demos/8.png" alt="Onboarding Matrix, Sole Prop / Single-Member LLC variant: a green tab pill above three green column headings, Business info, One representative, and Bank account. Five variants, one per business type: **Sole Prop · Single-Member LLC**, **Multi-Member LLC · Corp**, **Non-Profits**, **Government Entities**, and **Individuals**. Selecting a type swaps the variant, so the requirements are one component rather than five separate tables.

Because it is real text rather than an image, the requirements are readable by search engines and screen readers — which matters more here than on a decorative visual.

## The animation, and how it works

Each demo has roughly **20 or more animated elements**, staggered from about `0.07s` to `0.85s` and fully settled inside a second. The order tells a small story: the sidebar arrives, then the logo, then the menu items slide in one by one, then the stat tiles, then the body.

Two things to know before editing:

- **The entrance fires once, when the element scrolls into view.** Switching tabs does *not* replay it — that transition is Framer morphing between variants, which is why tab switching feels smooth rather than restarting.
- **Framer cannot animate height.** Bars that appear to grow from a baseline are actually sliding upward inside a frame with clipping turned on, each offset by exactly its own height so it starts fully hidden. If you resize a bar, update its animation offset too, or it will start partly visible.

## Editing safely

**Changing a number or a label** is safe — select the text and edit it. Do it on the primary variant where possible.

**Adding or removing a row** changes the height, which is fine, but check tablet width afterwards.

**Changing a color** should use the product color styles — JustPay green for Payments, JustGrow coral for Payables, JustMove blue for Beyond Payments. One rule worth preserving: **an avatar or status tint reflects that row's own state, not the demo's accent color.**

<Callout>
  <p><strong>Always check all three tabs after any edit to Product Demo.</strong> The three bodies are stacked siblings with two hidden, so it is very easy to edit the Payments body while thinking you are looking at Payables. The canvas will happily let you.</p>
</Callout>
