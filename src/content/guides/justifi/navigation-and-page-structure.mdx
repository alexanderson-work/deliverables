### CMS template pages

These three are **templates**, not pages. Each one is designed once and Framer generates a real page for every item in the matching collection.

| Template | Collection | Pages it generates today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/customers/:slug` | Customers | 3 |
| `/updates/:slug` | Updates | 14 |
| `/legal/:slug` | Legal | 3 |

Add an item to a collection and its page appears at the matching URL. See the [Using the Framer CMS](../using-the-framer-cms/) doc.

## The top navigation

The nav is a component called **Navigation Bar**, placed once in the layout template so it appears on every page. It has three variants: Desktop, Phone Closed, and Phone Open.

Its contents:

| Item | Type | Goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo | Link | `/` |
| Products | Dropdown | Payments, Payables, Beyond Payments, Explore Products |
| Resources | Dropdown | Why JustiFi, Developer Docs, Partner Hub |
| Log in | Button | — |
| Contact sales | Button | — |

The two dropdowns are instances of a **Nav Dropdown** component with a text control — the label ("Products", "Resources") is set per instance, so both share one design.

On phone, the nav collapses to a **Mobile Menu Button** that toggles between the Phone Closed and Phone Open variants.

## The footer

The footer lives in the layout template, not in a component. It has three link columns plus a legal row:

| Column | Links |
| --- | --- |
| **Products** | Payments, Payables, Beyond Payments — all to `/products` |
| **Resources** | Why JustiFi, Developer Docs (external), Partner Hub |
| **Trust & Support** | Trust Center (external), Security, Sub-Account Onboarding, Merchant Support (external) |
| **Legal** | Merchant Agreement, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy — all CMS-driven from the Legal collection |

## How to add a page to the navigation

1. Open the **Navigation Bar** component (Assets tab, or double-click any instance).
2. Select the **Desktop** variant.
3. To add a row to a dropdown, duplicate an existing row inside it and change the label and link.
4. Repeat inside the **Phone Open** variant — the mobile menu is a separate set of items and does **not** inherit from Desktop.
5. Preview at all three widths before publishing.

For the footer, do the same inside the layout template. Editing it there updates every page at once.

<Callout>
  <p><strong>The mobile menu is built separately from the desktop nav.</strong> Any nav change has to be made twice — once in the Desktop variant, once in Phone Open. It is the most common thing to forget.</p>
</Callout>

## Known gaps worth a decision

These are deliberate build-state notes, not bugs to panic about — but they should be resolved before or shortly after launch.

- **The three `/solutions/*` pages, `/customers`, and `/updates` are not linked from the top nav or the footer.** They are fully built and live at their URLs, but nothing on the site points to them yet. They need either a nav entry, a footer entry, or both.
- **The footer Products links point to bare `/products`** rather than the section anchors (`/products#payments`, `#payables`, `#beyond-payments`). The anchors exist on the page; the links just lost their fragments. Worth re-adding so the three links go to three different places.
- **Log in and Contact sales in the nav have no destination set.**
