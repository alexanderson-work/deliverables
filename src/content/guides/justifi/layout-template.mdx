## What's in the template

The template is a vertical stack with a Navy background and three children, in order:

- **Navigation Bar** — an instance of the nav component, pinned to the top of the viewport so it stays put as you scroll.
- **The content slot** — an empty placeholder. Whatever a page contains gets dropped in here.
- **The footer** — a Dark Navy panel, `24px` corner radius, capped at `1200px` wide, holding the three link columns and the legal row.

### Spacing

|  | Desktop | Tablet | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Page padding | `120px` top, `40px` sides and bottom | Same | `120px` top, `16px` sides and bottom |
| Gap between nav, content, and footer | `80px` | `80px` | `80px` |

The `120px` top padding is there to clear the fixed navigation bar. The footer switches from a horizontal row of columns on desktop to a vertical stack on phone.

## The page recipe

Every page follows the same shape inside the content slot:

- A single frame named **Content** — a vertical stack, `maxWidth: 1200px`, `width: 1fr`
- A `100px` gap between sections (the home page uses `60px`, because its sections carry more of their own internal padding)
- Each major section as a direct child of Content
- Sections separated by a `1px` divider with a gradient that fades out at both ends

That is the whole system. To add a section to a page, build a frame and drop it into Content at the right position — the spacing takes care of itself.

The home page additionally has two decorative layers sitting behind Content: a **Hero Gradient** and a **Dotted Pattern**. Both are positioned absolutely so they do not affect the stack.

## Editing the template

Open it from the **Assets** tab, or select any page and click through to the template from the right panel.

<Callout>
  <p><strong>Every change here lands on all 16 pages at once.</strong> Changing the footer, the top padding, or the background is a site-wide edit. Preview at all three breakpoints before publishing — template changes are the easiest way to break a page you were not looking at.</p>
</Callout>

The template has its own three breakpoints, independent of any page's. As with pages, edit Desktop first and let Tablet and Phone inherit.
